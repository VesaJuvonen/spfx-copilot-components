# Zava Revenue Deal Room - 10-minute business demo

## Audience and setup

For sales leaders, account teams, finance, solution specialists, customer success, and transformation sponsors. Use Contoso Global Expansion / `ZDR-2042` throughout. Start a fresh conversation and state that confirmed actions are session-only mock receipts.

## 1. Discover and qualify the opportunity

As Megan Bowen, prompt: **Where is the next growth opportunity in Contoso?**

Expected component: `DiscoverAccountOpportunity`.

Then prompt: **Should we pursue the Contoso expansion?**

Expected component: `QualifyOpportunity`.

Explain the product boundary: discovery organizes inspectable signals; qualification owns the human pursue/stage judgment. Do not claim autonomous lead creation or CRM stage changes.

## 2. Understand the account and prepare the meeting

Use `BuildAccountBrief` for sourced account context, then `PrepareCustomerMeeting` for one upcoming steering meeting. Contrast account-level relationship history with meeting-specific attendees, objections, proof, questions, and agenda.

Key message: the agent assembles the right evidence for the requested job instead of forcing a seller through CRM navigation.

## 3. Mobilize the buying committee

Prompt: **Map the buying committee for the Contoso expansion.**

Expected component: `MapBuyingCommittee`.

Show the portrait influence orbit and evidence list. Explain champion strength, missing economic authority, technical/security influence, legal involvement, and named relationship ownership. Expand into Deal Room to show the same deal with broader pursuit context.

## 4. Diagnose risk and build the close path

Use `GetDealRisk` to separate verified, contrary, stale, judgment, and inferred evidence. Continue with `BuildMutualActionPlan` and `TrackMeetingFollowUp` to show the portrait-backed buyer/seller runway and credible close range.

Be precise: the current showcase visualizes the runway and risk narrative. Full lifecycle editing, cross-route session propagation, and external persistence remain future implementation work where listed in `todo.md`.

## 5. Prove value and shape the proposal

Use `BuildValueCase`, `ShapeSolutionProposal`, and `ReviewProposalReadiness` to explain the intended separation between customer outcomes, solution proof, commercial alignment, unsupported claims, and readiness conditions.

Show the shared Draft/Evidence -> Review/Decision -> Confirm -> Receipt interaction pattern. State that secondary catalog tools share the polished host today; their complete domain-specific workflows remain tracked rather than overclaimed.

## 6. Model the commercial offer

Prompt: **Model a three-year ramped offer that protects 68% margin.**

Expected component: `SimulateCommercialOffer`.

Change quantity and discount. Show the recalculated contract value, gross margin, weighted forecast, authority, and labeled outcome contour. Explain why one control updates several compatible business consequences.

Then prompt: **Review the Contoso payment-term exception.**

Expected component: `ReviewDealException`.

Explain the governance boundary: simulation proposes; exception review owns conditions and a human decision.

## 7. Reconcile customer commitments

Use `ReviewMeetingCommitments` to describe item-by-item fact versus inference review. Continue to `TrackMeetingFollowUp` for owned commitments and due-state context. Never describe meeting inference as silently written to CRM.

## 8. Defend the forecast

Prompt: **Should Contoso remain in commit for Q4?**

Expected component: `InspectForecastCommit`.

Show buyer-confirmed evidence, seller judgment, stale assumptions, critical-path risk, and the human forecast decision boundary. Expand into Revenue Command. Use `ExplorePipelineQuality` to explain how one-deal evidence can later roll into leadership intervention without becoming another regional KPI dashboard.

## 9. Carry the sold outcome forward

Use `PlanCustomerSuccessHandoff` to connect sold outcomes, stakeholder continuity, milestones, risks, and first-value ownership. State explicitly that creating a handoff does not close the opportunity or write to a production system.

## 10. Make the full catalog discoverable

Prompt: **Explore what the Revenue Deal Room agent can do.**

Expected component: `ExploreAgentCapabilities`.

Search the 20 operational scenarios and select one result. Explain that the explorer advertises business outcomes and safe prompts, not technical tool names. Preview is read-only and applies no action.

## Business close

Copilot interprets intent and selects a bounded experience. The component supplies the inspectable visual, deterministic calculations, people, evidence, controls, and confirmation. Production CRM, CPQ, Graph, policy, and workflow systems can replace service implementations without redesigning the user experience.

## Guardrails and fallback

- Do not claim live Dynamics, Salesforce, Graph, CPQ, approval, or forecast writes.
- Do not claim model routing is proven by local screenshots; validate it in the tenant.
- Use the current publication screenshots when preview-host routing, CSP, or display mode is unavailable.
- Reset by starting a fresh conversation; session-store propagation is still an open implementation item.
