# Zava Innovation Hub - 10-minute business demo

## 1. Direction and participation

As Johanna Lorenz, frame Future of Onboarding with `LaunchInnovationChallenge`. Expand to Programs & Pilots and show growth and global participation. Explain why conversion and inclusion matter more than submission volume alone.

## 2. Idea creation and personal continuity

As Megan Bowen, use `SubmitInnovationIdea`, then `GetMyInnovation`. Show personal idea status, readiness, supporters, and the next gate. Continue into `BuildIdeaBusinessCase` and review investment, expected value, and evidence.

## 3. Governed triage

As Diego Siciliani, open `GetInnovationReviewQueue`. Compare Screening, Business case, and Pilot action buckets. Inspect completed Approved, Declined, and Sent back outcomes. Open one pending submission and demonstrate:

- Approve and advance
- Send back for evaluation with required rationale
- Decline with required rationale
- Consequence confirmation
- Semantic session receipt
- Updated action count

## 4. Investment governance

As Miriam Graham, use `TrackInnovationBudget` to explain allocated, committed, spent, forecast, and available capital. Open `ReviewInnovationFunding` for Smart Onboarding Journey and show that the person, not the model, owns the funding decision.

## 5. Experiment and value

As Johanna, use `ManageInnovationExperiment` to document hypothesis, threshold, evidence, and a go/pivot/stop recommendation. As Joni, use `TrackInnovationValue` to compare projected and realized outcomes.

## 6. Enterprise portfolio

Open `ExploreInnovationPortfolio` full screen. Review:

- Horizontal stage-gate progression
- Impact versus effort
- Horizon balance
- Conversion momentum
- Strategic theme performance
- Top performing ideas by verified value realized

Acknowledge one contributor and explain the evidence-based recognition model.

## 7. Executive communication and discovery

Use `GenerateInnovationBrief` for the leadership story. Finish with `ExploreAgentCapabilities` to show all 16 operational scenarios organized by business outcome.

## Business close

The sample modernizes the operating model rather than replacing governance. Copilot interprets intent and selects the bounded experience; components own records, calculations, evidence, controls, and confirmation. Production integrations replace services without redesigning the UX.
