# Zava Customer Resolution - 4-Minute Keynote

## Audience and setup

Audience: service, customer experience, and Microsoft 365 leaders. Start with a reset browser session and the six canonical conversation starters. The demo is offline; confirmations create session receipts only.

## Run of show

### 0:00-0:35 - One report, assembled context

Prompt: **Build a resolution plan for Alpine House's store activation issue.**

Expected tool: `BuildResolutionPlan`. Show the six-hour entitlement, 42 affected stores, verified firmware evidence, contrary network evidence, and editable plan. Say: “The agent assembles context; deterministic UX owns the decision.”

### 0:35-1:10 - Human correction changes the work

Open `DiagnoseCaseEvidence`. Reject the network hypothesis and point to the changed firmware confidence and promoted rollback step. Do not claim autonomous diagnosis.

### 1:10-2:00 - Expand into Resolution Room

Expand `BuildResolutionPlan`. Show the evidence-to-action canvas, SLA consequence clock, incident emergence, and named expert swarm. The exact case remains `ZCR-1048`.

### 2:00-2:40 - One case becomes an incident

Prompt: **Are today's activation cases isolated or a broader incident?**

Expected tool: `DetectServiceIncident`. Change the similarity threshold and inspect the contrary case. A person reviews any declaration.

### 2:40-3:25 - Recovery with empathy and authority

Prompt: **Review the recovery options for case ZCR-1048.**

Expected tool: `ReviewServiceRecovery`. Compare credit, extension, replacement, and no concession by cost, authority, precedent, and trust outcome. Enter Review; do not imply a production approval.

### 3:25-4:00 - Organizational learning

Open Service Operations. Show the demand river, projected global impact map, recurring-driver Pareto, and recovery matrix. Close on: “The customer reports the problem once; every role advances the same inspectable resolution.”
